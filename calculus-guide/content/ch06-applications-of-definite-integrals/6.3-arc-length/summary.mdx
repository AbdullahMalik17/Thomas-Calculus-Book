# Section 6.3: Arc Length

Determining the exact length of a curved trajectory by integrating infinitesimal hypotenuses $ds$.

---

## 1. Differential Arc Length $ds$

By the Pythagorean theorem:

$$
ds = \sqrt{dx^2 + dy^2} = \sqrt{1 + \left(\frac{dy}{dx}\right)^2} dx = \sqrt{1 + \left(\frac{dx}{dy}\right)^2} dy
$$

---

## 2. Standard Algebraic Technique

In textbook arc length problems, the radicand $1 + [f'(x)]^2$ is engineered to simplify into a perfect square trinomial:

$$
1 + [f'(x)]^2 = [p(x)]^2 \implies \sqrt{1 + [f'(x)]^2} = p(x)
$$
