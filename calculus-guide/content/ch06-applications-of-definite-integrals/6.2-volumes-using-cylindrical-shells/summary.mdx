# Section 6.2: Volumes Using Cylindrical Shells

The cylindrical shells method slices solids of revolution into thin cylindrical sleeves parallel to the axis of rotation.

---

## 1. Shell Method Formulas

- **Revolving about the $y$-axis** (integrating in $x$):
  $$
  V = 2\pi \int_a^b x \cdot [f(x) - g(x)] \, dx
  $$
- **Revolving about the $x$-axis** (integrating in $y$):
  $$
  V = 2\pi \int_c^d y \cdot [f(y) - g(y)] \, dy
  $$

---

## 2. Comparison: Shells vs Washers

- **Washers/Disks**: Slices are **perpendicular** to the axis of revolution.
- **Shells**: Slices are **parallel** to the axis of revolution.
