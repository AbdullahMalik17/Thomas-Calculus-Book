# Section 6.5: Work and Fluid Forces

Applications of definite integrals to physical mechanics: mechanical work, pumping liquids against gravity, and hydrostatic fluid pressure.

---

## 1. Mechanical Work

$$
W = \int_a^b F(x) dx
$$

For springs obeying Hooke's Law: $F(x) = kx$.

---

## 2. Work Done in Pumping Liquids

To pump liquid of weight density $\rho g$ out of a tank:

$$
W = \int_a^b (\text{Force}) \cdot (\text{Distance}) = \int_a^b [\rho g \cdot A(y) dy] \cdot (D - y)
$$

---

## 3. Hydrostatic Force

$$
F = \int_a^b \rho g \cdot h(y) \cdot w(y) \, dy
$$
