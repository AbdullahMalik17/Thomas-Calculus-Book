# Section 6.4: Areas of Surfaces of Revolution

When a smooth curve is rotated around an axis, it sweeps out a two-dimensional surface whose surface area is calculated by integrating frustum bands.

---

## 1. General Formulas

- **Revolving about the $x$-axis**:
  $$
  S = 2\pi \int y \, ds = 2\pi \int_a^b f(x) \sqrt{1 + [f'(x)]^2} \, dx
  $$
- **Revolving about the $y$-axis**:
  $$
  S = 2\pi \int x \, ds = 2\pi \int_a^b x \sqrt{1 + [f'(x)]^2} \, dx
  $$
