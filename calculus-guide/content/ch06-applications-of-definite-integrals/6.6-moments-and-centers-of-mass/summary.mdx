# Section 6.6: Moments and Centers of Mass

The center of mass or centroid represents the balance point of a physical or geometric system.

---

## 1. Centroid Formulas for a Lamina

For a region under $y = f(x)$ above the $x$-axis on $[a, b]$:

$$
A = \int_a^b f(x) dx
$$

$$
\bar{x} = \frac{1}{A} \int_a^b x f(x) dx
$$

$$
\bar{y} = \frac{1}{A} \int_a^b \frac{1}{2} [f(x)]^2 dx
$$

---

## 2. Pappus's Theorem for Volume

$$
V = 2\pi \bar{r} A
$$
